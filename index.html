01

Overview

Oinkonaut is a single-player arcade game that works entirely offline. It has no account system, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics, and it does not sell or share personal information.

02

Data we collect

None. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send any data from your device to us or to anyone else. Eureka Digital Labs (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us") does not collect, receive or store any personal information through the app.

03

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. Google Play, which you use to install and update the app, handles its own data under Google's privacy policy.

04

What stays on your device

Your progress — best altitudes, the local leaderboard of your top runs, coins, unlocked skins and upgrades, achievements, daily streak and settings — is saved in the app's own storage on your device. It is never transmitted. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

05

App permissions

  • Vibration — for haptic feedback during play (can be turned off in the game's menu).

The app may read the device's motion sensors for tilt controls (also switchable in the menu); this needs no permission and the readings never leave the game.

06

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

07

Your privacy rights

Eureka Digital Labs (Pty) Ltd is responsible for this policy. Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to complain to your local data protection authority (in South Africa, the Information Regulator). Because the app collects nothing, we hold no personal information about you, but you are welcome to contact us with any question.

08

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently (for example by adding advertising), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
